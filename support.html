Support

WidgiCalc for macOS 15 and later

Add the widget

  1. Open WidgiCalc once.
  2. Control-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
  3. Search for WidgiCalc and drag the widget onto the desktop. Clicking it instead adds it to Notification Center.

The widget looks grey or faded

macOS dims desktop widgets while another app is in front. To keep them in full color, open System Settings › Desktop & Dock and set Dim widgets on desktop to Never.

Change the style

Open the app and pick Graphite, Paper, Midnight, Retro or Forest under Style. The widget follows within a moment.

Copy a result

Click the display, in the widget or in the app, to copy the result. In the app, click any line in History to copy it.

Keys and rules

  • C clears the number you are typing; AC clears everything. The ⌫ key deletes the last digit.
  • Multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction: 2 + 3 × 4 = 14.
  • Percent after + or − is a percent of what comes before it: 100 + 15% = 115. Anywhere else it divides by 100.
  • Press = again to repeat the last operation.
  • Dividing by zero shows Undefined; press AC to start again.
