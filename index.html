A calculator on your desktop

WidgiCalc is a real calculator in a macOS widget. Click its keys right on your desktop or in Notification Center: no app to open, no window to find.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Works the way you expect

Multiplication before addition, percent that means what you say, repeat equals, and exact decimals: 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3.

Five styles

Graphite, Paper, Midnight, Retro and Forest. Pick one in the app and the widget follows.

A companion app

The same calculator in a window, with keyboard input and a history of your results.

Private

No data collected, no network connections. Everything stays on your Mac.
