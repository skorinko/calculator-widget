Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: WidgiCalc does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

Who we are

WidgiCalc is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the Mac App Store.

What the app stores

The app and its widget keep the calculator's current state, a history of your last results and your settings (style and thousands separators) in the app's own container on your Mac. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Network requests

WidgiCalc makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Clipboard

The app writes to the clipboard only when you click the display or a history line to copy a result. It never reads the clipboard.

Purchases

WidgiCalc is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

WidgiCalc collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: macapps.support@icloud.com.
